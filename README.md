# OSINT-Tools-Turkey
<img src="https://flagcdn.com/w320/tr.png" alt="Turkey_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Turkey (Türkiye). Most of the resources and tools has versions in Turkish; some also in English. Many personal and property lookups (land registry owner data, vehicle records, trade registry details in MERSİS) are available only to the data subject through e-Devlet (turkiye.gov.tr) with a Turkish ID number, and there is no public register of beneficial owners. The Trade Registry Gazette (TTSG), the Public Disclosure Platform (KAP), court decision databases and the Official Gazette remain the main open sources on companies and officials; appointments of senior officials are published in the Resmî Gazete. Enter names with Turkish letters (ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [TÜİK Veri Portalı (Turkish Statistical Institute Data Portal)](https://veriportali.tuik.gov.tr/) — official statistics: population, economy, prices, foreign trade and regional data. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [EVDS — Elektronik Veri Dağıtım Sistemi (Central Bank Electronic Data Delivery System)](https://evds3.tcmb.gov.tr/) — Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye time series: exchange and interest rates, monetary, banking and balance-of-payments data, with an API. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [Resmî Gazete (Official Gazette)](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/) — laws, presidential decrees, regulations and appointment and dismissal decisions of officials; archive since 1920. *Lang: TR.*
- [Mevzuat Bilgi Sistemi (Legislation Information System)](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/) — Presidency's database of legislation in force: laws, presidential decrees, regulations and communiqués. *Lang: TR.*
- [Yargıtay Karar Arama (Court of Cassation Decision Search)](https://karararama.yargitay.gov.tr/) — full-text search of Court of Cassation decisions in civil and criminal cases; needs JavaScript. *Lang: TR.*
- [UYAP Emsal Karar Arama (Precedent Decision Search)](https://emsal.uyap.gov.tr/) — Ministry of Justice's search of decisions of the regional appeal courts and selected first-instance courts. *Lang: TR.*
- [Danıştay Karar Arama (Council of State Decision Search)](https://karararama.danistay.gov.tr/) — decisions of the supreme administrative court, including tax and public-procurement disputes. *Lang: TR.*
- [Sayıştay — Raporlar (Court of Accounts — Reports)](https://www.sayistay.gov.tr/reports) — audit reports on ministries, municipalities, state-owned enterprises and other public bodies. *Lang: TR.*
- [Borsa İstanbul](https://www.borsaistanbul.com/) — Turkey's stock exchange: listed companies, indices, prices and market data. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [İBB Açık Veri Portalı (Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality Open Data)](https://data.ibb.gov.tr/) — open datasets of the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality: transport, budget, tenders and urban services. *Lang: TR, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [Türkiye Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi (Trade Registry Gazette)](https://www.ticaretsicil.gov.tr/) — Union of Chambers (TOBB) publication of company registrations, appointments of directors, capital changes, share transfers in limited companies, mergers and liquidations; search by trade name or registry number. Needs JavaScript. *Lang: TR.*
- [KAP — Kamuyu Aydınlatma Platformu (Public Disclosure Platform)](https://www.kap.org.tr/tr/sirketler/ALL) — disclosures of listed and other capital-market companies: shareholder structure, board members, subsidiaries, financial statements and material events; full-text search of disclosures and attachments. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [e-ŞİRKET (MKK Company Information Portal)](https://e-sirket.mkk.com.tr/) — Central Registry Agency portal for joint-stock companies: general assemblies, board members, articles of association and announcements. *Lang: TR.*
- [İTO Bilgi Bankası — Firma Bilgileri (Istanbul Chamber of Commerce Company Search)](https://bilgibankasi.ito.org.tr/tr/bilgi-bankasi/firma-bilgileri) — search of member companies of the Istanbul Chamber of Commerce: registry number, address and activity (NACE). *Lang: TR.*
- [SPK — Kurumlar (Capital Markets Board — Licensed Institutions)](https://www.spk.gov.tr/kurumlar/yatirim-kuruluslari/araci-kurumlar) — lists of brokerages, banks, portfolio managers, auditors, rating and valuation firms licensed by the Capital Markets Board; also publishes administrative sanctions and trading bans. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [Rekabet Kurumu — Kurul Kararı Arama (Competition Authority Decision Search)](https://www.rekabet.gov.tr/tr/Kararlar) — Competition Board decisions, including merger and acquisition approvals that describe ownership and control of the parties. *Lang: TR.*
- [Kamu Gözetimi Kurumu (Public Oversight Authority)](https://www.kgk.gov.tr/) — public registers of independent auditors and audit firms, and sanction decisions. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [TKGM Parsel Sorgu (Land Registry and Cadastre Parcel Query)](https://parselsorgu.tkgm.gov.tr/) — free query of land parcels by province, district, neighbourhood, block and parcel number or by clicking on the map: boundaries, area and land-use type. Owner data are not shown; Web Tapu gives owners access to their own records via e-Devlet. *Lang: TR.*
- [Harita İstanbul (Istanbul City Map)](https://harita.istanbul/) — Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality's map with address search, aerial imagery from different years, zoning and urban layers. *Lang: TR.*
- [Ankara Kent Rehberi (Ankara City Guide)](https://kentrehberi.ankara.bel.tr/) — Ankara Metropolitan Municipality's map with address and parcel search and municipal layers. *Lang: TR.*
- [İzmir Kent Rehberi (İzmir City Guide)](https://kentrehberi.izmir.bel.tr/) — İzmir Metropolitan Municipality's web GIS with address search, zoning and aerial imagery; needs JavaScript. *Lang: TR.*
- [MTA Yerbilimleri Harita Görüntüleyici (Earth Sciences Map Viewer)](https://yerbilimleri.mta.gov.tr/) — General Directorate of Mineral Research and Exploration's geological maps, active faults, mineral deposits and mining sites. *Lang: TR.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [SBM — Sigorta Bilgi ve Gözetim Merkezi (Insurance Information and Monitoring Centre)](https://www.sbm.org.tr/) — operator of TRAMER, the motor insurance claims database: damage history of a vehicle by plate or chassis number via paid SMS to 5664 or the Sigortam360 app, and compulsory insurance status. *Lang: TR.*
- [Platesmania — Turkey](https://platesmania.com/tr/search) — crowdsourced photos of vehicles with Turkish plates; search by plate. *Lang: TR, EN.*

### Maritime
- [Denizcilik Genel Müdürlüğü — Gemi Sicil (Directorate General of Maritime Affairs — Ship Registry)](https://denizcilik.uab.gov.tr/gemi-sicil) — information on the national and Turkish International Ship Registers (TUGS), vessel types eligible for registration and authorised municipalities for small craft; no public vessel search. *Lang: TR.*
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [SHGM — Tescil İşlemleri (Directorate General of Civil Aviation — Aircraft Registration)](https://web.shgm.gov.tr/tr/hava-araci-islemleri/2205-tescil-islemleri) — rules of the Turkish Civil Aircraft Register (TC- marks): reservation of registration marks, registration and deregistration; the register itself is not published online. *Lang: TR.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [TBMM — Milletvekilleri (Grand National Assembly — Members)](https://www.tbmm.gov.tr/milletvekili/liste) — current and former MPs with biographies, party, constituency and contact details; also parliamentary records search. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [Yüksek Seçim Kurulu (Supreme Election Council)](https://www.ysk.gov.tr/) — official election results, candidate lists and party registrations. May be unreachable from foreign IP addresses. *Lang: TR.*
- [Türkiye Barolar Birliği — Avukat Arama (Union of Turkish Bar Associations — Lawyer Search)](https://www.barobirlik.org.tr/AvukatArama) — search of lawyers registered with Turkish bar associations. Blocks automated access. *Lang: TR.*
- [YÖK Ulusal Tez Merkezi (National Thesis Centre)](https://tez.yok.gov.tr/UlusalTezMerkezi/) — database of master's and doctoral theses from Turkish universities, searchable by author, supervisor and subject. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [YÖK Akademik (Academic Search)](https://akademik.yok.gov.tr/) — profiles of academics at Turkish universities: positions, education, publications and projects. Blocks automated access. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/) — TÜBİTAK platform hosting Turkish academic journals; search of articles and authors. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [Gaste Arşivi](https://www.gastearsivi.com/) — digitised archive of historical Turkish newspapers, searchable by date and title. Blocks automated access. *Lang: TR.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [EKAP — Elektronik Kamu Alımları Platformu (Electronic Public Procurement Platform)](https://ekapv2.kik.gov.tr/ekap/search) — Public Procurement Authority's search of tenders, the Public Procurement Bulletin, results and contracts under Law 4734. *Lang: TR.*
- [Kamu İhale Kurumu (Public Procurement Authority)](https://www.kik.gov.tr/) — regulator: board decisions on procurement complaints, the list of debarred bidders and procurement statistics. *Lang: TR.*
- [ilan.gov.tr](https://www.ilan.gov.tr/) — Press Advertising Agency's official announcements portal: tenders, auctions of seized property, real estate sales and other legal notices. *Lang: TR.*
- [Devlet Malzeme Ofisi (State Supply Office)](https://www.dmo.gov.tr/) — central purchasing body for public institutions: catalogue, framework purchases and supplier lists. *Lang: TR, EN.*

## WHOIS
- [TRABİS — Alan Adı Sorgu (.tr Domain Search)](https://www.trabis.gov.tr/whois) — official .tr registry lookup operated by the Information and Communication Technologies Authority (BTK) since 2022. *Lang: TR, EN.*
- [IANA — .tr Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/tr.html) — official delegation data for .tr: registry operator, contacts and name servers. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Turkish networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
